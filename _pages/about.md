I am a Postdoctoral Researcher (Research Scholar) in Building Science at the School of Architecture, University of Southern California (USC). My research focuses on Human-Building Integration — advancing environmental sustainability, energy-efficient building operation, and occupant comfort and well-being through real-time multi-occupant sensing and intelligent thermal comfort control.

I received my Ph.D. in Architecture from Chung-Ang University in 2023, where my dissertation developed a computer vision-based occupant-centric thermal control framework using real-time metabolic rate and clothing insulation estimation.
